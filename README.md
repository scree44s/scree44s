## Hi there 👋
## My name is Tyler (or Scree, whichever you prefer)!
## 😄 Pronouns: He/Him

## I am an indie programmer and game designer; my primary languages are UE Blueprints (occassionally C++), PureData, Basic (+formulas), Python, and HTML (+CSS).

## I am strongly against the use of AI/LLMs in Art or Design projects; the human experience is trial and error, and is beautiful. LLMs get rid of that.
